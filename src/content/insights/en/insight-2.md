Most business applications were designed for humans clicking through screens. AI agents need a different interface: structured capabilities, clear permissions, tool descriptions, and reliable responses.

## Why AI Needs Structured Access

When an agent only sees a human-facing page, it has to infer what the product can do. That creates room for mistakes. With MCP-style interfaces, applications can expose actions in a way agents can discover, understand, and use safely.

This helps AI systems call the right tool, pass the right inputs, and return better results to the user.

## How Companies Benefit

AI-ready interfaces can improve support automation, internal operations, customer onboarding, analytics workflows, and product integrations. They also make your business easier to interact with through future assistants and agent marketplaces.
