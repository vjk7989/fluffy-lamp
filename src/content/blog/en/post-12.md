    'Private AI deployment is the architecture decision behind a simple question: where can sensitive data safely go? For many companies, the answer is no longer a single public API. Legal, security, and customer requirements increasingly push AI workloads toward private cloud, on-prem infrastructure, local-first devices, or carefully routed hybrid systems.',
    'Private cloud is often the fastest step up from public AI APIs. It gives teams stronger network boundaries, enterprise contracts, regional controls, and centralized monitoring while preserving managed infrastructure. The trade-off is cost and continued dependence on an external provider.',
    'On-prem AI gives the organization maximum control over hardware, data location, and network egress. It can be the right choice for defense, healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, or companies with strict data residency needs. The operational cost is higher because the team owns capacity planning, upgrades, observability, and incident response.',
    'Local-first AI keeps workspace data, tool access, and context on the user machine. This works well for desktop assistants, developer workflows, internal research, and sensitive document work because the system can use local storage while routing only selected tasks to approved models.',
    'Hybrid routing is usually the most practical long-term pattern. Low-risk requests can use fast cloud inference, sensitive tasks can stay local or on-prem, and high-accuracy work can move through governed private endpoints. The routing policy becomes a security control, not just a performance feature.',
    'PAVii.AI designs private AI deployments around data boundaries first: what must stay local, what can enter private infrastructure, what can reach public models, and how every decision is logged. That clarity lets businesses adopt AI without forcing every workflow through the same risk profile.'
