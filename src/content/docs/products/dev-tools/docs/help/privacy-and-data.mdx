## On your computer

- Your code, files and terminals.
- The activity graph in Settings > Profile: minutes, sessions and tokens per day for the last year, stored only here.
- Your settings, themes and custom agents.

## Signed in or not

Skipping sign-in keeps every other feature working. Signing in is for account features, such as Token saving, the Observability engine and PII masking, and for phone and Telegram pairing.

## With PII masking

In the complete application, personal data is masked before a prompt leaves your computer. A blockchain-backed record proves what was masked and when, without holding the data. Details in [Privacy and blockchain-backed masking](/products/dev-tools/docs/how-it-works/privacy-and-masking/).

:::note[Beta and invite-only]
Not every feature here is in the current early-access app. The complete application is invite-only, by email invitation.
:::
