Channels connect your assistant to the chat apps you already use. Messages
sent there reach the same assistant, agents, and workspace as the desktop
composer.

## Supported channels

- **Telegram**
- **Slack**
- **Discord**

## Setting up a channel

1. Open **Settings → Channels**.
2. Pick a channel and follow its pairing flow — each channel walks you through
   connecting a bot to your account or workspace.
3. Bind the channel to an assistant. Messages arriving on that channel are
   answered by the assistant you chose.

## What to expect

- Conversations started from a channel appear in your history like any other
  conversation.
- The app runs the agents locally — your desktop (or a machine running
  `pavii-web`) needs to be on for channel messages to be processed.
