Most systems today are made for people, not for AI. AX (Agentic Experience) makes your application easy for AI agents to understand and use. It gives your business an interface for agents. Agents can then find what your application does and call the right tools.

## Key ideas

- **MCP (Model Context Protocol).** An open standard that lets agents use tools and data.
- **A2A (Agent2Agent).** An open protocol that lets agents talk to other agents.
- **Agent.** A program that uses an AI model to take actions, such as reading data or calling a tool.

## How it works

1. You connect your product, website or internal tool to AX through MCP and A2A.
2. AX turns your services, data, actions and workflows into context that AI can use.
3. Agents find the main functions of your application. Each function has clear inputs, outputs and limits.
4. Pavii checks each agent call at six security gates. Your rules decide which actions each agent can do.
5. Pavii records every action, so you can see what each agent did.

## Why it matters

- Your core application does not need a rebuild. AX adds a layer on top of your current systems.
- Agents get structured access. They do not guess from screens that were made for people. This means fewer wrong actions.
- Your users can work with your product through AI assistants and still get correct results.

## Who it is for

AX suits SaaS products, service businesses, internal platforms and websites. Any product that you want AI agents to use is a good fit.
