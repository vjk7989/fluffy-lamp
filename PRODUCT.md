# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, technical teams, businesses, and individual knowledge workers evaluating PAVii.AI products and AI infrastructure. Visitors need to understand the product quickly, see credible product evidence, and download the correct desktop build without ambiguity.

## Product Purpose

Present PAVii.AI as a confident, model-agnostic AI infrastructure and agent platform. The website should explain what PAVii does, demonstrate real product capabilities, and turn interest into product exploration, downloads, or contact with the team.

## Brand Personality

Confident, practical, collaborative. PAVii should feel technically capable without becoming cold, and ambitious without making unsupported claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template branding, leftover ScrewFast identity, repetitive SaaS card grids, vague AI imagery without product evidence, tiny low-contrast copy, and download experiences that hide platform limitations or installation requirements.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a clear user outcome before infrastructure terminology.
- Show the real product through screenshots and working downloads.
- Keep primary actions unmistakable and platform-specific.
- State compatibility, trust, and installation limitations honestly.
- Preserve PAVii's blue-led identity and direct, collaborative voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Respect reduced-motion preferences, retain visible focus states, use semantic controls, and never rely on color alone to communicate download state or platform requirements.
